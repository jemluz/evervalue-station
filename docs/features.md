# 🎨 Features

## Overview

### **Essentials**

- ✅ Universal conversion panel: BTC ↔ Satoshi ↔ USD ↔ BRL ↔ EVA
- ✅ EVA price in real time (USD and BRL)
- ✅ EVA conversion to Satoshis and BTC

### **Extras**

- 📊 Total supply, burned, and circulating tokens
- 👥 Holder count
- 💰 Market cap and 24h volume
- 💧 Liquidity in main pools
- 🧮 Investment calculator

## Stages / Phases

### **P0 - Essentials (MVP)**

1. **Universal Converter**
   - Input: any currency (BTC, USD, BRL, EVA, Satoshi)
   - Output: conversion to all others
   - Real-time updates

2. **Price Panel**
   - EVA price in USD
   - EVA price in BRL
   - Value in Satoshis
   - Value in BTC

### **P1 - Extras (V1)**

3. **Supply Metrics**
   - Total supply
   - Burned tokens
   - Circulating supply
4. **Holder Metrics**
   - Total holders
   - Growth rate (optional)

### **P2 - Future (V2)**

5. **Market Data**
   - Market cap
   - 24h volume
   - Liquidity
6. **Investment Calculator**
   - "Invest R$ X -> receive Y EVA"
   - "If EVA reaches $X, my investment is worth Y"
